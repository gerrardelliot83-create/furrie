## In plain English

Before L1, a customer with no credits could walk through the whole booking flow and — depending on a hidden Vercel setting — get a free consultation or an error on the last step, and three more code paths could hand out free consultations or packs. Now:

- **Booking needs a credit.** Without one, the customer is offered the buy screen instead; no vet slot is held and no vet is disturbed. Taking the credit and confirming the booking happen in one database step, so a credit can't be lost halfway.
- **Buying is self-serve.** 1 / 3 / 5 / 10 consultations, price + 18% GST (₹589 / ₹1,651 / ₹2,595 / ₹4,719). The customer pays the exact amount to the founder's personal UPI (phone: UPI-app button; computer: QR code; always: copy buttons for UPI ID, amount, reference), then taps **I've paid** — or **Send payment details on WhatsApp**, which marks it paid and opens WhatsApp to +91 89779 22442 with the details and a link to the order in the admin portal. **Call** is next to it.
- **Admins grant in one click** after finding the payment in the bank, or close the order if it isn't there (optionally emailing the customer). Every grant and close is in the audit log.
- **Invites work from the sign-up screen** (the code box accepted only a paste), and the free credit shows on the first dashboard view. **Waitlist members** get a 60-day founding credit.
- The payment-gateway routes are switched off (decision D4).

## Deviations from the plan (all approved by Gerard on 2026-09-25 unless noted)

- **GST on top** (plan had GST-inclusive list prices). 18% and rounding to whole rupees are **assumptions awaiting Gerard's confirmation** — one constant each in `src/lib/pricing/packs.ts`.
- **No account-holder name notice**; the pay screen says "This is our founder's personal UPI".
- **WhatsApp + Call** added to the pay and checking screens; the WhatsApp message carries an admin link that needs admin sign-in and grants nothing.
- **Alias blocking (G6) not built** — deferred until after all tests (Gerard tests with +aliases). Backlog L1-1.
- **Founding list arrives as CSV** (the waitlist is in another Supabase project — found in the snapshot), via two ops scripts rather than one.
- A dedicated **`/buy` page** (planned as a panel only) so emails can link to it.
- "Payment not found" email to the customer is **off by default** in the close dialog (the admin ticks it).
- The pay screen's credit-arrival promise is deliberately **time-free** until Gerard says who checks the bank and when (`PAYMENT_CHECK_PROMISE` in `src/lib/upi/config.ts`).
- Hidden legacy UI (`ConsultationRequestModal`, pack purchase components) is now unused but **not deleted** (D6) — backlog L1-2.
- Not in the original plan, done because it sat in L1's files: invite share texts (no testimonial, no banned "fur baby"), `GET /api/packs?status=` for the parked mobile app, "Being confirmed" label for `pending`.
